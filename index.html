Our point of view

A wardrobe should feel collected, not consumed.

Attire no•II is an independent vintage edit founded in California by fashion designer and curator Nisa Kaya. We source expressive, wearable pieces and give them a thoughtful second life.

No endless inventory. No trend treadmill. Just singular clothes, selected slowly.
